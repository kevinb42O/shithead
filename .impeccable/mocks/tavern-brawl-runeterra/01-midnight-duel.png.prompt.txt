Use case: ui-mockup. Create a finished high fidelity desktop browser card battle screen for TAVERN BRAWL, edge to edge 16:10 landscape, no device or browser frame. This is an original fantasy card game with anthropomorphic fox, owl, raven and hare characters. Player is the Fox versus the Owl. Preserve the existing game: two opposing rows of exactly three crew places, one hero per player, a fanned hand, energy, a shared coin pot, End turn. No extra combat lanes or new rules.

Ground is deep ink teal, highlights restrained champagne gold and ivory. Premium painterly fantasy illustration, strong silhouettes, dramatic lighting and layered depth; the sophistication and game feel of Legends of Runeterra, with entirely original characters and interface.
